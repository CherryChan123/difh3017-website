🌱 After Reading

This story has no ending, because attention difficulties are usually not a problem that gets "solved." They may come and go, and may change with sleep, stress, environment, and physical condition.

If you recognised yourself in some of Ah Ching's moments, that does not mean you have any "disorder." It only means you are a person, trying to focus in a world designed to make focusing hard.

If you want to talk to someone, see Find Help. If you want to know what the data can and cannot say, see What We Know.
