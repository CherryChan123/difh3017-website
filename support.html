Find Help

If you have read the previous pages and feel like talking to someone, here are some places to start.

🆘 If you need immediate support

"Emotional Support" Mental Health Hotline
Health Bureau · 24 hours
Phone / WhatsApp: 18111
Service: 24-hour staffed phone and WhatsApp support, providing immediate assistance and counselling
Languages: Cantonese, Mandarin, English, plus several ethnic minority languages
The Samaritans · 24-Hour Emotional Support Hotline
The Samaritans
Phone: 2389 2222
Service: 24-hour emotional support, answered by volunteers who listen and accompany callers through difficulties
Samaritans · 24-Hour Multilingual Suicide Prevention Hotline
The Samaritans
Phone: 2896 0000
Service: 24-hour multilingual emotional support

💬 If you prefer text or online chat

"Open Up" Jockey Club Youth Emotional Health Online Support Platform
The Hong Kong Jockey Club Charities Trust · Subvented by the Social Welfare Department
WhatsApp / SMS: 9101 2012
Service: 24-hour online counselling, suitable for youth aged 6–24
Format: Contact via WhatsApp or SMS; no need to speak directly
The Samaritans · "Chat Room" Online Counselling
The Samaritans
Hours: Daily, 4:00 p.m. to 1:00 a.m.
Format: Online text counselling

🎓 If you are a university student

University Student Counselling Services
Student Affairs Offices of various universities
Most Hong Kong universities offer free, confidential student counselling. You can book through the Student Affairs Office website or visit the counselling centre directly.
HKBU students: Contact the Counselling and Development Centre at the Student Affairs Office, or the Chaplaincy's counselling service (free for full-time students)

🏥 If you want longer-term, non-urgent professional support

Fu Tak Shan Counselling and Development Centre
The Mental Health Association of Hong Kong
Service: Individual counselling, psychological assessment, cognitive behavioural therapy, provided by clinical psychologists and counsellors
Address: Flat F, 9/F, Kam Man House, 337–339 Nathan Road, Kowloon (Jordan MTR Station Exit A)
Phone: 3583 1196
Hours: Mon, Tue, Thu 2:00–10:00 p.m.; Wed, Fri 9:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m.; Sat (alternate weeks) 9:00 a.m.–1:00 p.m.
Hospital Authority Mental Health Hotline
Hospital Authority
Phone: 2466 7350
Service: 24-hour psychiatric hotline providing mental health consultation and support, with referral to HA psychiatric services
For: Psychiatric rehabilitation service users, family members, and the general public

📌 A few practical reminders

  • You do not need to "prove" you have a problem before seeking help. If you feel like talking, you can talk.
  • Hotline volunteers are not doctors. They will not diagnose you, but they can listen and help you organise your thoughts.
  • If no one answers your call, try another number, or call again later. It is not your fault.
  • University counselling services usually require an appointment, but in an emergency you can call or visit in person.
  • This page may be updated over time. If a number does not work, check the latest information on the Social Welfare Department or the Education Bureau's "Campus Positive" website.
